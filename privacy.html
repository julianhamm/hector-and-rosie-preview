A little discretion…

Hector & Rosie is about making introductions, and that relies on a degree of trust.

When you introduce yourself, the information you share about your family and your work is intended to appear on the site. Your email address, however, remains private and won't be published.

If you'd like to meet another parent, your note comes to me. I'll use it to arrange an introduction, sharing contact details where appropriate and with permission.

I won't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

If you'd like to amend or remove your introduction, or have any questions about the information I hold, simply drop me an email at julian@hectorandrosie.com.

Julian Hamm

A few practical details

I'm responsible for the personal information collected by Hector & Rosie. Introductions and requests are stored using Google Sheets and Apps Script; email is handled using Cloudflare and Gmail. I use your information to review and publish introductions and respond to requests, based on your consent to publication and my legitimate interest in running the service.

I aim to keep personal information only for as long as needed for these purposes. You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information, object to processing or withdraw consent where applicable. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
